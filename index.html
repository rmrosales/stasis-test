DRILLING EVOLVED™

Stasis leads the evolution of Managed Pressure Drilling (MPD), delivering closed-loop, automation-driven MPD solutions that set a new standard in pressure control, well performance, and operational certainty.

 

Our technologies go beyond conventional services, enabling real-time pressure intelligence, reduced NPT, and a smarter, more autonomous drilling operation.

OUR OFFERINGS

EQUIPMENT RENTALS & SERVICES

Fast-deploy MPD equipment and field-ready support built
for reliable pressure control operations.

ENGINEERING SERVICES

Hydraulics modeling, pressure analysis, and operational planning
tailored to complex drilling environments.

RIG INTEGRATIONS

Custom MPD integration solutions designed
to work seamlessly with existing rig systems.

PRODUCTS

Purpose-built MPD equipment, control systems, manifolds, and rotating control devices engineered in-house.

MPD TRAINING

IADC-accredited training programs focused
on real-world operations, safety, and
pressure management.
